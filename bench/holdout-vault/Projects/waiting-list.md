# Waiting list

About 120 people are waiting for a plot. Today the list is a paper folder at the gate and a spreadsheet Hana keeps on her laptop.

Moving it online needs:

- the new privacy notice approved by the committee, because we store names, emails and phone numbers;
- a way for people without email to stay on the list (Hana will add them by hand);
- a yearly "are you still interested?" email. People who do not answer within 30 days are moved to an inactive list, not deleted.

Order is strictly by join date ([[02_DECISIONS]], 7 July). When a plot frees up, the first three people are offered it at the same time and the first to accept gets it.
