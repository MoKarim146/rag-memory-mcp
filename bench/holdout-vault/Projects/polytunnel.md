# Polytunnel bookings

The polytunnel is shared. Groups (the primary school, the mental health charity, the seed swap) want to book it for sessions.

- Bookings are in half-day blocks: morning 08:00 to 13:00 or afternoon 13:00 to 18:00.
- The heater runs on a timer; Dev will read the bookings and set the heater schedule automatically next year.
- Open question: can children's groups book on weekdays during term time? The committee meets on this in November.

Each block has a maximum of 12 people inside, for fire safety.
