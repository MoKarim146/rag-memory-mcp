# Watering rota

In summer the shared beds and the polytunnel need watering every day. Plot holders sign up for two-hour slots between 06:00 and 22:00.

## Slot swap

Two plot holders can trade slots. Rules agreed with Hana on 21 October:

1. Both slots must be in the same calendar week.
2. A bank holiday slot can only be swapped for another bank holiday slot.
3. Both people must accept within 48 hours, otherwise the swap request expires.

The swap is stored as a pair of rows in `rota_swaps` so it can be undone by a committee member.

## Calendar

The calendar shows a week at a time. After the accessibility check, the "free slot" green was too pale; Lucia changed it from #9BE7A0 to #1E7B34 so the text passes contrast.

## Winter

From November to February the rota is paused and only the polytunnel needs a weekly check.
