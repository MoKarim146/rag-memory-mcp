# Style guide

## Brand

- Fernhill green #2F5D3A for buttons and links. Cream #F6F1E7 for backgrounds.
- Headings use the Fraunces font; body text uses the system font stack.

## Writing

- British English: colour, favourite, organise.
- Short sentences. Say "order", not "purchase".
- Prices always with a pound sign and two decimals: £3.50, never £3.5.
- Dates in copy: Tuesday 4 November, without the year unless it is next year.

## Buttons

- Verbs first: "Add to basket", "Pay now", "Collect from Roath".
- Never use "Click here".
