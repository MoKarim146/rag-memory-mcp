# Glossary

- Click and collect window: the 30-minute slot a customer picks for collecting an order from a shop.
- Cut-off: 14:00 the day before pickup or delivery. Orders after that go to the next available day.
- Core zone: delivery postcodes with free delivery over £25.
- Night bake: the overnight bake, planned at 15:00 and baked from 22:00.
- Drop: one delivery stop for the van.
- Outward code: the first part of a UK postcode, for example CF11.
- Box: one week of the sourdough subscription.
