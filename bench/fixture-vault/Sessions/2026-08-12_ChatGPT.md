
## 2026-08-12T11:02:47.000Z - Timezone bug in the date picker

Model: ChatGPT

Mina reported that orders placed after 23:00 showed a pickup date one day early in the admin screen. Cause: the date picker sent a JavaScript Date, the API converted it to UTC, and during British Summer Time local midnight is 23:00 UTC the day before. Fix: send and store plain YYYY-MM-DD strings, and compute "tomorrow" in Europe/London on the server. Added tests for orders placed at 23:30.
