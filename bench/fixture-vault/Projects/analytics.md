# Analytics

We self-host a small privacy-friendly analytics service on the VPS. No cookies and no personal data, so there is no cookie banner.

## What we track

- Page views per page, with the referrer domain only.
- Funnel: product page, basket, checkout started, paid.
- The language toggle, to see how many customers use Welsh.

## What we do not track

- No IP addresses are stored. No user ids. No session replay.

## Numbers so far (staging test week)

- 41 percent of test users who started checkout finished paying. Most drop-offs happened on the delivery postcode step.
