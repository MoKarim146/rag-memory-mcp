# Design review, 14 April 2026

Present: Ines, Kofi.

We reviewed the trail page and the recording screen with five beta testers on a video call.

- Three testers could not find the "start recording" button with gloves on. The button is now 72 pixels tall and sits at the bottom of the screen.
- Everyone wanted the remaining distance and the estimated time to the end of the trail in big text.
- Two testers hike with their phones in airplane mode to save battery. Recording must work in airplane mode: GPS still works without a network.

Decisions: minimum iOS 15 and Android 10 for now; self-host Sentry because crash reports contain coordinates.
