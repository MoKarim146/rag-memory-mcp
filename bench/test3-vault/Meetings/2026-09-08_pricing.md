# Pricing review, 8 September 2026

Present: Ines, Sam.

Numbers since subscriptions launched on 4 August:

- 2,140 trials started, 312 converted to paid (14.6 percent).
- Of those who converted, 91 percent did so in the first 7 days of the 14-day trial.
- 80 percent of paying users chose the yearly plan.
- Server and map costs per paying user are about £1.10 a month.

Agreed:
- Shorten the free trial to 7 days for new users.
- Keep the monthly price at £3.99, raise the yearly price from £24.99 to £29.99 for new subscribers. Existing subscribers keep their price.
- Ines will update the paywall copy to make the yearly saving clearer.
