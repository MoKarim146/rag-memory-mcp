# Beta feedback review, 21 July 2026

Present: Ines, Kofi, Sam.

Feedback from 112 beta testers over six weeks:

- Most loved: offline maps that just work, and the big distance-to-go number.
- Most requested: a way to tell someone where you are if something goes wrong. 31 testers asked for it in some form. This became emergency share.
- Biggest complaint: the app forgot recordings when the phone ran out of battery. Kofi fixed it in 1.2.3 by writing points in batches of 20 instead of keeping them in memory.
- Several testers recorded routes from their front door, so routes should be private by default.

Crash-free sessions: 99.1 percent in the last two weeks.
