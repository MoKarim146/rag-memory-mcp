# Kickoff, 10 March 2026

Present: Ines, Kofi, Sam.

Ines explained the idea: a hiking app that works fully offline, for UK hikers first. Competitors either need a signal or hide offline maps behind an expensive plan.

Kofi proposed React Native with Expo so we can ship both platforms from one codebase. Sam was fine with that and suggested Firebase for the backend to move fast. We agreed to revisit the backend once we know our costs.

Scope for the first beta: download a region, follow a trail, record a route. No accounts in the first week of testing.

Beta testers: 40 people from two hiking clubs, recruited by Ines.
